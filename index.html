STUDIO WORKFLOW

The Four Pillars of Beat Making

Master every stage of turning a raw melody loop into a radio-ready master inside SOLO STEPPIN CRETE STUDIO.

1. Sound Design & 808s

Shape punchy sub-bass harmonics, pitch-envelope kicks, and lush analog synth pads that cut cleanly through mobile and club speakers.

2. Drum Bounce & Pocket

Layer syncopated hi-hat rolls, off-grid percussion rims, and hard-hitting snares to create undeniable head-nodding groove.

3. Sampling & Arrangement

Chop soulful loops, build tension with riser transitions, and structure 8-bar hooks and verses that keep artists inspired.

4. Mixing & Master Bus

Carve stereo width with panning, balance low-end headroom, and glue your mix together with soft-clipping saturation.

Master of orchestral trap melodies, thunderous sub-808 patterns, and razor-sharp hi-hat bounce that defined modern Atlanta hip-hop.

Neptunes Bounce & Funk

Iconic 4-count intro tags, minimalist syncopated percussion, and futuristic synth chords that bridge hip-hop, R&B, and pop.

Dreamy Video-Game Plucks

Pi'erre Bourne

Pioneer of ethereal synth loops, nostalgic video-game arpeggios, and bouncy 808 slides that shaped melodic trap and rage soundscapes.

Gengetone & Afro-Club Drums

Magix Enga

Kenyan hitmaker and Beat King renowned for high-energy Gengetone rhythms, infectious log-drum percussion, and club-shaking basslines.

EarDrummers founder famous for speaker-rattling low end, hypnotic vocal chops, and stadium-ready Southern trap anthems.
